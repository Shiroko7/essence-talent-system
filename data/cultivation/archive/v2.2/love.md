# Love Cultivation Path

Tradition: Divine / Godhood
Concept: Love, beauty, passion, emotional bonds, and the power to kindle or turn affection.

## Initiate Tier

### Attraction/Disdain

```yaml
id: ruin_initiate_attraction_disdain
tier: initiate
isActive: true
isPassive: false
isSpell: false
isCantrip: false
author: Cyric (source technique; emotional manipulation)
```

**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S, M (a holy symbol)  
**Duration:** 1 hour  
**Portfolio:** Emotional influence

Choose attraction or disdain when you cast this spell:

**Attraction.** You touch a creature, which must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failure, any negative emotions it has toward you become positive emotions (hatred becomes love, etc.). Your Charisma score increases by 1d4+2 for interactions with the target.

**Disdain.** You touch a creature and name another creature it knows. The target must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failure, any positive emotions it has toward the named creature become negative emotions. The named creature's Charisma score is reduced by 1d4+2 for interactions with the target.

If the target's emotions are extremely intense, the DM can impose a +1 to +5 bonus to the saving throw.
