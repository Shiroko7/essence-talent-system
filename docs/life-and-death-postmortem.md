# Life and Death: why the first draft was removed

**Status: removed.** A Life and Death path was drafted as an Immortal path on 2026-11-03 and removed the same day. This note records what went wrong, so a future attempt starts from the right place.

## What was asked for

Players had long asked for "life and death" as one thing, not a Life path and a Death path. The path was meant to be about **the cycle**: techniques themed solely on life, others solely on death, the cycle between them, and rebirth. Souls are only a small part of that, and affecting a soul directly shouldn't be possible before Grandmaster.

## What the draft did instead

The draft treated the path as "control over the veil" and made nearly everything about souls:

- **Sense the Threshold** (Initiate): tell at a glance whether a creature is hurt, dying, or dead, and whether its body holds its soul. Players can already ask the DM this, or roll a check for it.
- **Hold the Threshold** (Initiate, reaction): a creature that drops to 0 hit points drops to 1 instead. Far too strong for a tier every character can dip into.
- **Loosen the Soul** (Initiate): pull at a creature's soul. Mechanically weak, but targeting a soul directly is a Grandmaster idea, not an Initiate one.
- **Catch the Soul** (Adept): just another version of Hold the Threshold, and uninspiring.
- **Send It On** and **Spirit Walk** (Adept): too specific to earn a place.
- **Life for Life** (Initiate): added a modifier on top of doubled healing, which was unnecessary.
- Its spell list put soul spells (Séance, Unhouse the Soul, Magic Jar, Soul Cage, Spirit Lantern, Little Death, Life Thread) well below the Grandmaster tier.

## Lessons for a future attempt

1. Start from the cycle: some techniques about life (vitality, growth, birth, renewal), some about death (decline, the ending), some about how one feeds the other, and rebirth at the top.
2. Keep soul effects at Grandmaster or above.
3. Initiate techniques are free dips for every character (see the technique design rules in [`cultivation-paths-design.md`](cultivation-paths-design.md)), so nothing that cheats death belongs there.
4. Use Pathfinder 2e vitality and void spells as references for actives and passives.
5. Healing and resurrection spells were kept out of V2 for balance. They only come back if the path has a cycle-based reason for them.

## What was undone

The path file, its generated module, its palette, icon, domain (The Threshold), and its matchup with Crypt were removed. Spare the Dying and Death Ward, which had moved to it from Providence, are back in Providence with their original IDs.
