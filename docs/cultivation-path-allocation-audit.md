# V2 Path Allocation Audit

**Purpose:** record the thematic allocation of every V2 custom talent, cantrip, and leveled spell, documenting cross-path reallocations and the porting of missing V1 abilities. All approved decisions are implemented in the canonical V2 path source files (`data/cultivation/v2/`).

The catalog contains **361 entries**: 185 custom talents (121 active, 64 passive, as tagged in source), 39 cantrips, and 137 leveled spells. Every entry appears below with its original home, audit recommendation, and finalized implemented decision.

**Confidence:** High = the described effect strongly belongs to the recommended path; Medium = the placement is reasonable but has a meaningful adjacent-path reading; Low = placement depends on interpretation, needs rewriting, or has no clean current home.

**Decision column:** records the finalized, implemented destination path for each entry in V2.

## Review flags and catalog questions

- **Sunlight currently sits in Lunar:** the local descriptions for *Light*, *Guiding Bolt*, and *Sacred Flame* explicitly say sunlight/solar radiance. The first two are recommended for a future Sun path if one is added; until then Providence is only a temporary home. If you want either in Lunar, rewrite its presentation as moonlight. The standard 5e *Light* spell describes an object shedding light and does not define it as sunlight ([D&D Beyond: Light](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2166-light)).
- **Broad shape-changing in Alchemy:** *True Polymorph* and *Shapechange* are general transformations, not specifically chemical or alchemical processes. The local *True Polymorph* summary narrows it to a “plague beast,” unlike the spell’s broad creature/object transformation ([D&D Beyond: True Polymorph](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2284-true-polymorph); [D&D Beyond: Shapechange](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618982-shapechange)). I recommend Alchemy only if broad transmutation belongs to that path; otherwise revisit/remove.
- **Love versus coercion:** *Suggestion*, *Compulsion*, *Dominate Person*, and *Mass Suggestion* affect agency but do not inherently express affection. The report leans toward Ruin for coercive use, while marking the placement Low where the spell could be benevolent. *Geas* is tentatively Pestilence because curses are part of that portfolio. Your intended boundary between Love, Ruin, and Pestilence will decide these.
- **Sky versus Tempest:** the report sends the localized sound/pressure effects *Thunderclap* and *Thunderwave* to Sky, while leaving large weather systems and storm-calling effects in Tempest. The overlap is real; this is the main elemental boundary to confirm.
- **Metal’s broad utility spells:** *Animate Objects* only clearly belongs in Metal if limited to metal objects. *Arcane Aegis* has only a source link and no local rules text, so I could not judge its actual imagery or effect. *True Strike* is assigned to Ruin only if its custom “murder insight” framing is intentional.
- **Unassigned candidates:** *Weight of Lives*, *Ethereal Phase Barrier*, and *Frozen Insight* have effects that do not clearly match their current paths; each is marked for revisit instead of receiving a forced fit. *Etherealness* is kept in Shadow only conditionally, if that path includes travel through a veil between planes.
- **Heart remains spell-less:** its Stone Fist progression and Radiant Vein forms are treated as human martial techniques. *Entangling Reach* and *Coiling Resilience* are recommended for Heart because their mechanics are grappling-based and the text does not currently establish a plant/root effect.
- **Active/passive tag mismatches:** *Glacial Shield*, *Tide’s Reflection Art II*, *Tide’s Reflection Art III*, and *Entangling Reach* are tagged passive despite requiring a reaction, bonus action, or action. *Explosive Savant* and *Shadow Veil* also grant activated options while remaining passive features, so their mixed behavior may be intentional. The 97/60 counts above follow source flags and should be revisited after those tags are settled.
- **Local name/effect mismatch:** *Thunderstep* in Sky creates a thunderclap and stuns nearby creatures; it does not teleport. Consider renaming or rewriting it when you review that row.
- **Metadata issue:** Earth’s *Earthen Ward* has **tier: active**, although its flags mark it as an active custom talent. Its progression tier should be checked separately from its path placement.
